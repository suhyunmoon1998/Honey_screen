# Risk Register

| ID  | Risk                                                                                              | Probability | Impact   | Mitigation                                                                                                                | Owner                  |
| --- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ----------- | -------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ---------------------- |
| R1  | Daily-cap race condition allows an 11th substantive question under concurrency                    | Medium      | High     | Use transaction-scoped advisory locking, unique daily interaction constraints, and integration stress tests               | Engineering            |
| R2  | Staff authorization bug exposes another client or organization’s data                             | Medium      | Critical | Central actor-context authorization, repository scoping, audit events, and negative authorization matrix in CI            | Engineering            |
| R3  | Service worker accidentally caches authenticated responses                                        | Medium      | High     | Offline shell only, explicit `no-store`, SW route exclusion, and cache inspection browser tests                           | Engineering            |
| R4  | OTP flow leaks account existence or is brute-forced                                               | Medium      | High     | Generic responses, per-IP and per-account throttling, hashed single-use codes, and dev-provider production guard          | Engineering            |
| R5  | Evidence becomes accessible before a clean scan result                                            | Low         | Critical | Quarantine-first storage, fail-closed scanner state machine, clean-only signed URLs, and integration tests                | Engineering            |
| R6  | Legal content in DRAFT state is served in production                                              | Low         | Critical | Production approval gate, audited emergency override only, and production-config tests                                    | Engineering + Legal    |
| R7  | Notification text leaks employer or case details on lock screens                                  | Medium      | High     | Generic templates only, redaction tests, and review of all channel copy                                                   | Product + Engineering  |
| R8  | Staff authorization is incorrectly inferred from Google login success or email domain alone       | Medium      | Critical | Require database allowlist plus explicit role assignment and test STAFF vs ADMIN boundaries                               | Engineering            |
| R9  | Malware scanner integration exists structurally but production is launched without a real scanner | Medium      | High     | Fail closed when no real scanner is configured and keep all non-CLEAN files inaccessible                                  | Engineering + Security |
| R10 | PWA install telemetry is overstated, misleading staff workflows                                   | Medium      | Medium   | Store confidence-semantics events separately and use precise UI language                                                  | Product + Engineering  |
| R11 | Typed bilingual copy coverage drifts and English/Spanish parity breaks                            | Medium      | Medium   | Central typed catalogs, lint/test checks for missing keys, and locale smoke tests                                         | Product + Engineering  |
| R12 | Prisma abstractions are insufficient for concurrency-critical SQL paths                           | Low         | Medium   | Allow parameterized raw SQL only for advisory locks and worker-claim queries; keep those queries isolated and tested      | Engineering            |
| R13 | Local development becomes hard to run because too many external services are required             | Medium      | Medium   | Use Docker Compose for DB/MinIO, dev-only adapters for OTP and notifications, and safe local defaults                     | Engineering            |
| R14 | Staff workflow scope expands into case management rather than intake review                       | Medium      | Medium   | Keep milestone scope aligned to product spec, explicitly defer non-MVP case-management features                           | Product                |
| R15 | Logging or error reporting captures PII through defaults                                          | Medium      | High     | Redaction-first logging helpers, request-body logging ban, and synthetic PII regression tests                             | Engineering + Security |
| R16 | Time-zone changes could be abused to seek extra same-day budget                                   | Medium      | High     | Derive local date from stored IANA zone, audit every change, and ensure existing same-day ledger rows remain counted      | Engineering            |
| R17 | Content workflow clones stale branching or review rules into a new draft without reviewer notice  | Medium      | Medium   | Display source version, audit draft creation, and require admin approval before any new version is client-visible         | Product + Engineering  |
| R18 | Operational telemetry could drift into security-audit retention or expose sensitive content       | Medium      | High     | Keep `OperationalEventSink` separate from `AuditEvent`, emit content-free schemas only, and assert redaction in tests     | Engineering + Security |
| R19 | Fictional demo question content could be loaded outside dev/test                                  | Low         | Critical | Require `ALLOW_DEMO_CONTENT=true`, fail closed in production, and keep legal production content on a separate import path | Engineering + Legal    |
| R20 | Forward migration from Task 01 data could regress resumability or historic answer associations    | Medium      | High     | Run `pnpm db:verify-upgrade` against a populated Task 01-era database before closeout                                     | Engineering            |
